Refund & Cancellation

Please contact Herbiveda before cancelling an order, course booking or retreat enquiry. Refund and cancellation eligibility depends on the product or programme, its booking terms, preparation or dispatch status, and any supplier or retreat-centre conditions communicated at the time of purchase.

For a refund or cancellation request, email healing@herbiveda.com or call +91 80 76 75 70 22. Include your order or booking reference and the reason for your request. Herbiveda will review the request and communicate the applicable next steps.

For product quality or damaged-item concerns, contact Herbiveda promptly and retain the product packaging and order details so the matter can be reviewed.

Any specific refund amount, processing timeline or cancellation charge will be confirmed after reviewing the applicable order or booking terms.
