Description & Evidence

Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is a classical Ayurvedic rasayana traditionally used for vitality, stress resilience and sleep. Modern clinical research — including multiple randomised controlled trials and meta-analyses — has examined its effects on perceived stress, anxiety scores, sleep quality and cortisol-related markers. Herbiveda selects batches for standardised quality and provides certificates of analysis so you (and your practitioner) can see exactly what is in each lot.

Every recommendation on this site is labelled honestly as traditional use or clinical evidence and is reviewed by a licensed practitioner before publication.
