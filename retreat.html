Who This Retreat Is For

This retreat is for guests seeking a thoughtful Ayurvedic wellness experience in Kerala—especially those who want to slow down, enjoy nourishing food, explore traditional therapies, and build more balanced daily routines.

✓ This is for you if…

  • You want doctor-led, classical protocol — not à la carte massages
  • You can commit 14 days without a sightseeing agenda
  • You value daily clinical check-ins and evidence-minded care
  • You prefer a small, quiet cohort over a busy resort

✗ Not for you if…

  • You want a beach resort with optional spa therapies
  • You need nightlife, tours, or a packed social calendar
  • You cannot follow dietary and rest guidelines during the stay
  • You prefer a purposeful wellness rhythm rather than a resort-style holiday

Before you apply

Common questions

How intense is a 14-day programme?

It is rest-heavy: oil therapies, a restricted diet, and limited stimulation. It is not a sightseeing trip. Guests who need nightlife or packed tours are a poor fit.

Can I book a single room?

Yes, subject to availability. Single occupancy is usually a higher rate than shared. We’ll confirm options when we quote.

When is the best season in Kerala?

Monsoon (roughly June–September) is traditionally favoured for many oil therapies. October–March is drier and often preferred if you combine the retreat with travel. We’ll match timing to your goals.

Deposit, cancellation, and who should not attend?

A deposit is typically required to hold a seat after you accept a quote. Cancellation terms depend on the centre and timing. Programme suitability can be discussed privately before booking — please keep the first form focused on your travel plans and wellness preferences.
