Before you apply

Common questions

How intense is a 14-day programme?

It is rest-heavy: oil therapies, a restricted diet, and limited stimulation. It is not a sightseeing trip. Guests who need nightlife or packed tours are a poor fit.

Can I book a single room?

Yes, subject to availability. Single occupancy is usually a higher rate than shared. We’ll confirm options when we quote.

When is the best season in Kerala?

Monsoon (roughly June–September) is traditionally favoured for many oil therapies. October–March is drier and often preferred if you combine the retreat with travel. We’ll match timing to your goals.

Deposit, cancellation, and who should not attend?

A deposit is typically required to hold a seat after you accept a quote. Cancellation terms depend on the centre and timing. Medical contraindications are reviewed on consultation — please do not include sensitive clinical detail in the first form.
